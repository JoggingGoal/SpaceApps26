How are the scores calculated?

For each crop, a documented screening profile estimates relative sensitivity to rainfall shortfalls and hot-day anomalies. A crop-family diversity calculation uses Simpson's index and transitions between botanical families. Soil texture and adoption complexity are estimated separately. The final index is a weighted combination averaged across historical weather seasons with extra weight on challenging seasons.

Important: It does not validate crop phenology, seed variety, irrigation water balance, pests, planting windows, yields, or profits. Unknown species are evaluated with broad family assumptions and explicitly flagged. NASA POWER is regional weather, not a field measurement.

Educational screening tool, not a validated crop-suitability, planting, yield, or profit forecast. Confirm the crop variety, calendar, local markets, equipment, water supply, pests, and soil tests with an agronomist. NASA POWER data is gridded regional weather; MODIS vegetation is not a farm-boundary measurement.
